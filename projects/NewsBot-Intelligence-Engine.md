# NewsBot Intelligence Engine 2.0

Repository: [NewsBot-Intelligence-System-2.0-ITAI2373](https://github.com/icheftech/NewsBot-Intelligence-System-2.0-ITAI2373)

## Problem Statement

News moves faster than most analysts can manually review. The goal of NewsBot is to transform raw articles into structured intelligence that can be searched, summarized, categorized, and explored.

## Approach

- Build a FastAPI service for ingestion and analysis.
- Use LDA for topic modeling and pattern discovery.
- Use DistilBART for abstractive summarization.
- Use SentenceTransformers for semantic similarity and retrieval.
- Provide an interactive Gradio interface for exploration.

## Results and Evaluation

The project demonstrates a complete NLP workflow from raw text to decision-ready outputs. Evaluation should include representative summaries, discovered topics, search examples, and screenshots or tables from the Gradio interface.
