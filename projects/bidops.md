# bidops

Repository: [bidops](https://github.com/icheftech/bidops)

## Problem Statement

Contract sourcing requires teams to monitor opportunity portals, evaluate fit, and route promising bids quickly. Manual review can miss relevant opportunities and slow response time.

## Approach

- Use a modern web application for opportunity review.
- Combine backend APIs with agentic workflow logic.
- Integrate SAM.gov-style opportunity intelligence.
- Store and rank procurement data for repeated sourcing decisions.

## Results and Evaluation

The project demonstrates an AI-native business workflow that connects data ingestion, opportunity scoring, and operational routing. Evaluation should include sample opportunities, scoring logic, workflow screenshots, and deployment notes.

## Dependencies

Next.js, TypeScript, FastAPI, LangGraph, PostgreSQL, and deployment configuration from the repository.

## Learning Outcomes

- Learned how to apply AI systems to business development.
- Practiced connecting agent workflows to persistent data and user-facing dashboards.
- Improved full-stack architecture and deployment planning.
