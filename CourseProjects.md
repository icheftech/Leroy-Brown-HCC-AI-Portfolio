# Course Projects

This document summarizes the portfolio projects and connects each project to its source repository, documentation, and technical focus.

| Project | Repository | Problem Statement | Approach | Technologies | Learning Outcomes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| NewsBot Intelligence Engine 2.0 | [GitHub](https://github.com/icheftech/NewsBot-Intelligence-System-2.0-ITAI2373) | News data is too large and fast-moving for manual review. | Built an NLP pipeline for classification, topic discovery, summarization, sentiment, and semantic exploration. | FastAPI, LDA, DistilBART, SentenceTransformers, Gradio | Learned how to combine classical NLP and transformer models into an interactive intelligence workflow. |
| SSO Control Plane | [GitHub](https://github.com/icheftech/sso-control-plane) | AI systems in regulated settings need governance, auditability, and emergency controls. | Designed workflow registries, policy gates, control policies, break-glass paths, and hash-chained audit events. | Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, SQLAlchemy, NIST AI RMF | Learned how responsible AI practices become enforceable software architecture. |
| bidops | [GitHub](https://github.com/icheftech/bidops) | Procurement teams need faster discovery and scoring of relevant contract opportunities. | Built an AI-native sourcing workflow with opportunity ingestion, routing, scoring, and bid intelligence. | Next.js, FastAPI, LangGraph, Railway Postgres, SAM.gov | Learned how to apply agentic workflows to real business development operations. |
| PMBC Connect / Triberolls | [GitHub](https://github.com/icheftech/pmbc-connect) | Churches need a modern system to manage member engagement, events, and care workflows. | Built a deployed member-management app with practical dashboard and record workflows. | JavaScript, React/Next.js, Netlify | Learned how to translate community operations into usable software interfaces. |
| CloudArch | [GitHub](https://github.com/icheftech/CloudArch) | Cloud operations can be repetitive, error-prone, and difficult to coordinate manually. | Prototyped an autonomous cloud orchestration layer with planning workflows and infrastructure adapters. | LangGraph, Terraform adapter patterns, Python | Learned how AI agents can assist infrastructure planning while still requiring controlled execution. |
| Geoffrey | [GitHub](https://github.com/icheftech/Geoffrey) | Household operations involve many recurring tasks that can benefit from context-aware automation. | Designed an AI household manager with task planning, recognition, and API-driven automation. | FastAPI, LangGraph, facial recognition | Learned how multimodal and agentic systems can support personal operations when privacy is considered early. |
